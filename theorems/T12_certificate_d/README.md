## 📐 The theorem in the program

Certificate D is the universal μ4-theorem: [L,P]=0 — the Lefschetz and polarization operators commute — with functoriality over Z[i]. The monograph proves commutativity at the level of integral lattices, which is what allows every later certificate to transport structure between rungs without re-proving it. The two counter-rotating rings of the motif are the two operators, locked in permanent commutation.
