## 📐 The theorem in the program

Certificate B closes the kernel-29 blind spot: a period combination that numerically shadowed the boundary is shown to vanish identically, and the 29+2 obstruction collapses to zero. The monograph gives the closing computation in exact arithmetic and records the protocol row that the stand replays on demand. After B, the boundary of the K3 arithmetic is clean by construction, not by tolerance.
